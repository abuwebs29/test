How to use it

Start with the amount on your bill

1. Find the annual charge

Use the full-year amount issued by your local council, before any discount you want to test.

2. Select a reduction

Choose 25%, 50%, a full exemption, or enter a custom percentage shown on your notice.

3. Compare instalments

See the equivalent payment over 10 or 12 months and recalculate the remaining balance.

Important: Discounts and exemptions must normally be applied for through your local council. Continue paying the amount on your official bill until the council confirms a change.
Common questions

Council Tax FAQ

Why does the calculator not ask for my postcode or band?

Council Tax charges differ by local authority, parish and property band. Entering your issued annual charge avoids presenting an inaccurate national rate.

Can I pay over 12 months?

Council Tax is often billed over 10 months. Contact your council if you want to request payment over 12 months.

Who may qualify for a 25% discount?

A full bill assumes at least two counted adults. You may qualify when you live alone or everyone else in the home is disregarded for Council Tax purposes.

Does this replace my official bill?

No. It is an estimate only. Your local council determines the amount due, eligibility for reductions and payment dates.
