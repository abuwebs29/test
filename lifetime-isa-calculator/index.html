How the rules affect the projection

Opening age

You normally need to make your first Lifetime ISA payment before age 40.

Bonus age limit

Eligible contributions and government bonuses stop at age 50, although the account can continue to grow.

First-home use

A qualifying first home must usually cost £450,000 or less and be bought at least 12 months after the first payment.

Common questions

Lifetime ISA FAQ

Does the £4,000 Lifetime ISA limit sit inside the main ISA allowance?

Yes. Lifetime ISA contributions count towards the overall annual ISA allowance.

Can I use a Lifetime ISA for any first home?

The property generally needs to cost £450,000 or less, be bought with a mortgage, and the account must have been open for at least 12 months.

Why does a 25% withdrawal charge lose more than the bonus?

Because the charge applies to the whole amount withdrawn, including your contribution, the government bonus and any growth.

Can I keep the account after age 50?

Yes. You cannot normally add more money or receive further bonuses after 50, but the account can remain open and continue earning interest or investment returns.
