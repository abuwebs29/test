Common questions

Emergency Fund FAQ

Should I include discretionary spending?

Usually the target focuses on unavoidable costs rather than holidays, entertainment or optional subscriptions that could be reduced during an emergency.

What if I have no monthly contribution?

The calculator will show the funding gap but cannot estimate a completion date until a positive monthly contribution is entered.

Should I invest my emergency fund?

Emergency savings are generally intended to be accessible and stable. Investments can fall in value and may not be suitable for urgent short-term needs.

Does the calculator save my figures?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser.
