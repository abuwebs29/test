Calculation guide

What this estimate includes

Taxable profit

Turnover minus allowable business expenses. Expenses cannot reduce profit below zero in this simplified model.

Income Tax

2026/27 Personal Allowance and regional non-savings income bands, including the allowance taper above £100,000.

Class 4 NI

6% on self-employed profits between £12,570 and £50,270, then 2% above £50,270.

Important: This simplified estimate excludes student loan repayments, Capital Gains Tax, dividend and savings tax rules, Marriage Allowance, High Income Child Benefit Charge, loss relief, accounting adjustments and many special cases. Confirm the final figure with HMRC software or a qualified adviser.
Common questions

Self Assessment FAQ

When is the 2026/27 online return normally due?

For the tax year ending 5 April 2027, the normal online filing and balancing-payment deadline is 31 January 2028.

When do payments on account apply?

They normally apply when the relevant amount owed is at least £1,000 and less than 80% of the total tax was collected outside Self Assessment. HMRC calculates the official amount.

Does Class 2 National Insurance appear here?

No mandatory Class 2 charge is added. Some people with low profits may choose voluntary Class 2 contributions to protect their National Insurance record.

Can I use this for a limited company?

No. A company director may have Corporation Tax, payroll, dividend and other obligations that need a different calculation.
