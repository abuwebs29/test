How the estimate works

2026/27 Statutory Paternity Pay rules

Weekly rate

£194.32 or 90% of average weekly earnings, whichever is lower.

Leave length

Eligible employees can usually take one or two weeks of statutory paternity leave.

Earnings threshold

Average weekly earnings must normally be at least £129.

Important: This calculator estimates gross statutory pay before Income Tax, National Insurance and pension deductions. Eligibility also depends on employment history, relationship to the child and notice requirements.
Common questions

Statutory Paternity Pay FAQ

Is paternity pay taxed?

Yes. Statutory Paternity Pay is normally paid through payroll and is subject to PAYE deductions.

Can the two weeks be taken separately?

Current rules may allow eligible leave to be taken as one block or two separate one-week blocks, subject to timing and notice requirements.

Can an employer pay more?

Yes. An employer can offer enhanced contractual paternity pay, but not less than the statutory amount for eligible employees.

What if earnings are below £129 per week?

You may not qualify for Statutory Paternity Pay, although you may still qualify for statutory leave depending on your circumstances.
