How it works

Understanding the estimate

Monthly target

The calculator solves for the regular monthly contribution needed after allowing the current balance to grow.

Time to target

In timeline mode, it compounds the balance monthly and adds each contribution until the target is reached.

Interest contribution

Estimated interest is the final balance minus current savings and all future contributions.

Common questions

Savings goal FAQ

What happens if my current savings already exceed the target?

The calculator shows that the goal is already reached and no further monthly contribution is required.

Can I use a 0% interest rate?

Yes. The calculator then divides the remaining target across the available months or estimates the timeline using contributions alone.

Does it account for tax on savings interest?

No. Tax depends on the account type, your Personal Savings Allowance and your circumstances.

Should I use this for investments?

For market-based investments, use the Investment Return Calculator because investment returns fluctuate and may be negative.
