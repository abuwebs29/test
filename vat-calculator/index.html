How it works

VAT formulas

The correct formula depends on whether your starting amount includes VAT.

Add VAT

VAT amount = net amount × VAT rate

Gross amount = net amount + VAT amount

Remove VAT

Net amount = gross amount ÷ (1 + VAT rate)

VAT amount = gross amount − net amount

Common questions

VAT Calculator FAQ

How do I add VAT to a price?

Multiply the net amount by the VAT rate to calculate the VAT, then add the VAT amount to the net amount.

How do I remove VAT from a total?

Divide the VAT-inclusive amount by one plus the VAT rate. At 20%, divide the gross amount by 1.2.

Why is removing 20% VAT not the same as subtracting 20%?

Because the gross total already represents 120% of the net amount. Subtracting 20% of the gross amount therefore gives the wrong net figure.

Does this calculator store my figures?

No. The calculator runs locally in your browser and does not require an account.
