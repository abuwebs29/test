How the calculator works

The wall area is calculated from the room perimeter multiplied by wall height. Door and window area is subtracted, then ceiling area is added when selected. The result is multiplied by the number of coats and divided by the paint coverage per litre. Your chosen extra allowance is then added.

Tips for a better estimate

Measure each wall separately for irregular rooms. New plaster, porous surfaces and dramatic colour changes may need extra paint or a primer. It is usually sensible to retain a small amount for later touch-ups.

Frequently asked questions

How much area does one litre of paint cover?

Coverage differs by product. Many interior paints advertise roughly 10–14 square metres per litre per coat, but the label on your chosen paint is the best source.

Should I subtract doors and windows?

For a more precise estimate, subtract their combined area. For a quick estimate, leaving them included can provide a useful allowance.

Does the result include multiple coats?

Yes. Set the number of coats and the calculator multiplies the paintable area accordingly.
