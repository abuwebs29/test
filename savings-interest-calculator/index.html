Common questions

Savings Interest FAQ

What is compound interest?

Interest is added to the balance, allowing future interest to be earned on deposits and earlier interest.

Does this calculator include tax?

No. Results show gross interest before tax and do not assess the Personal Savings Allowance or tax-free ISA treatment.

When are monthly deposits added?

The estimate assumes deposits are made at the end of each month.

Will my actual rate stay the same?

Not necessarily. Easy-access and variable-rate accounts can change, while fixed accounts may restrict deposits or withdrawals.

Are my savings details stored?

No. Everything is calculated locally in your browser.
