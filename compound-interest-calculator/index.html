Common questions

Compound Interest FAQ

What is compound interest?

It is growth earned on the original amount and on growth already added in earlier periods.

Is this an investment forecast?

No. It is a mathematical illustration using a constant assumed return. Actual returns can be higher, lower or negative.

How are fees handled?

The entered annual fee is subtracted from the assumed annual return before the monthly projection is calculated.

What is inflation-adjusted value?

It estimates what the future balance may be worth in today's purchasing-power terms.

Are my figures stored?

No. Everything is calculated locally in your browser.
