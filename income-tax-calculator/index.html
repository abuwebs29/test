How it works

Rates and assumptions

This calculator estimates Income Tax only and uses simplified annualised 2026/27 assumptions.

Personal Allowance

The standard allowance is £12,570.

It reduces by £1 for every £2 of adjusted income above £100,000.

England, Wales and NI

Taxable income is charged at 20%, 40% and 45% across the bands used by this calculator.

Scotland

Scottish earned income uses 19%, 20%, 21%, 42%, 45% and 48% bands in this estimate.

Common questions

Income Tax Calculator FAQ

What Personal Allowance does this calculator use?

It uses the standard £12,570 Personal Allowance and reduces it by £1 for every £2 of adjusted income above £100,000.

Does this include National Insurance?

No. This page estimates Income Tax only. Use the National Insurance Calculator for employee NI.

Does Scotland use different tax bands?

Yes. Select Scotland to apply the Scottish earned-income tax bands used in this 2026/27 estimate.

Why might HMRC calculate a different amount?

Your tax code, benefits, pension contributions, reliefs, multiple income sources and PAYE timing can all affect the actual amount.

Are my income details stored?

No. The calculation runs locally in your browser and does not require an account.
