How the mortgage payment is calculated

The calculator uses the standard amortisation formula. Each monthly payment includes interest plus repayment of part of the original loan. At higher rates or longer terms, total interest usually increases.

What does loan-to-value mean?

Loan-to-value (LTV) is the mortgage amount divided by the property price. For example, a £240,000 mortgage on a £300,000 property has an 80% LTV.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include stamp duty?

No. Stamp duty and other buying costs are separate from the mortgage calculation.

Can I use it for buy-to-let?

You can explore repayment amounts, but many buy-to-let products are interest-only and use different affordability rules.

Why can a lender quote a different payment?

Lenders may use different compounding, fees, payment dates, product periods and rounding.
