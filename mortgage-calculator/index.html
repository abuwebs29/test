How it works

Mortgage repayment formula

The calculator uses the standard amortisation formula for a repayment mortgage with monthly payments.

Mortgage amount

Property price − deposit

This is the amount borrowed before fees.

Monthly repayment

The payment is calculated from the loan balance, monthly interest rate and total number of payments.

Total interest

Total repaid − mortgage amount

A higher rate or longer term usually increases total interest.

Estimate only: This calculator excludes lender fees, valuation costs, insurance, stamp duty, changing interest rates, product transfers, overpayments and early repayment charges.
Common questions

Mortgage Calculator FAQ

What does loan-to-value mean?

Loan-to-value, or LTV, is the mortgage amount divided by the property price. A £240,000 mortgage on a £300,000 property has an 80% LTV.

Does this include stamp duty?

No. Stamp duty and other property-buying costs are separate from this mortgage calculation.

Can I use this for buy-to-let?

You can explore repayment amounts, but many buy-to-let products are interest-only and use different affordability rules.

Why might a lender quote a different payment?

Lenders may use different compounding methods, fees, payment dates, introductory product periods and rounding.

Are my mortgage details stored?

No. The calculation runs locally in your browser and does not require an account.
