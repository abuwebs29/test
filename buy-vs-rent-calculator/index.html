What this model includes

Buying path

Deposit, purchase costs, mortgage payments, maintenance, property growth, remaining debt and selling costs.

Renting path

Rent increases plus investment growth on the deposit, buying costs and any monthly cash-flow advantage.

End position

The comparison focuses on estimated net assets after the selected period rather than monthly cost alone.

Important: This is a simplified illustration. It excludes insurance, service charges, leasehold costs, taxes on investments, mortgage product fees, renovation costs and the non-financial benefits of either option.
Common questions

Buy vs rent FAQ

How is the renter's investment balance calculated?

The model invests the deposit and buying costs, then adds any monthly amount by which renting is cheaper than owning.

What if owning is cheaper each month?

The model reduces the renter's investment contribution by the difference but does not allow withdrawals below zero.

Does the calculator predict house prices?

No. Property growth is an assumption you control. Actual prices can rise or fall.

Should I decide based only on the result?

No. Flexibility, security, location, maintenance responsibility and future plans are also important.
