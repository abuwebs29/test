How the rate is chosen

2026/27 company car bands

Zero emissions

Cars with 0g/km CO2 use a 4% appropriate percentage.

1–50g/km

Plug-in hybrids use a 4% to 16% rate depending on their official zero-emission range.

51g/km and above

The percentage rises in 1-point steps across 5g/km bands, reaching the 37% maximum at 155g/km.

Important: This estimate does not cover company fuel benefit, optional remuneration arrangements, classic cars, shared cars or every adjustment HMRC may require. Check the vehicle's official CO2 and electric-range figures.
Calculation method

How the estimate works

1. Car value

List price and taxable accessories less a qualifying employee capital contribution, capped here at £5,000.

2. Taxable benefit

The adjusted value is multiplied by the appropriate percentage and reduced for the days the car was unavailable.

3. Income Tax

Qualifying private-use payments are deducted, then your selected marginal tax rate is applied.

Common questions

Company car tax FAQ

Should I use the price I paid for the car?

No. Company car benefit normally uses the manufacturer's list price when new, including taxable accessories, rather than the discounted purchase or lease price.

What counts as zero-emission mileage?

For cars emitting 1–50g/km, use the official electric range from the certificate of conformity, leasing company or fleet provider.

How does the diesel supplement work?

A 4 percentage point supplement normally applies to diesel cars that do not meet Euro 6d/RDE2, but the total percentage cannot exceed 37%.

Does this include employer Class 1A National Insurance?

No. The result estimates the employee's Income Tax cost, not the employer's Class 1A National Insurance liability.
