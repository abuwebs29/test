How the estimate works

Income-based repayments

The calculator subtracts the relevant annual threshold from gross annual income, applies 9% for an undergraduate plan and adds 6% above £21,000 when a Postgraduate Loan is selected.

Payroll differences: Employers calculate deductions for each pay period and normally round deductions down to whole pounds. Bonuses, overtime, multiple jobs and Self Assessment can therefore produce results that differ from this annualised planning estimate.
Common questions

Student Loan FAQ

Does the outstanding balance change the deduction?

No. Repayments are normally based on income and plan type rather than the amount owed. The Student Loans Company may move borrowers close to repayment onto Direct Debit.

Can undergraduate and postgraduate deductions happen together?

Yes. If income exceeds both thresholds, the 9% undergraduate-plan deduction and 6% Postgraduate Loan deduction can both apply.

What happens if income falls below the threshold?

Mandatory deductions stop for pay periods below the relevant threshold and restart when income rises above it.

Does this estimate interest or payoff time?

No. It estimates income-based deductions only. Interest, balance changes and write-off dates depend on plan-specific circumstances.

Are my figures stored?

No. Everything is calculated locally in your browser.
