How to use the result

Common questions

Budget Planner FAQ

Should I enter gross or net income?

Use monthly take-home income after tax, National Insurance, pension deductions and student loan repayments.

How should I handle annual bills?

Divide the annual amount by 12 and add the monthly equivalent so the budget reflects the cost throughout the year.

What does a negative balance mean?

Your entered outgoings are higher than income. Review flexible costs first, then consider whether essential bills, debts or income need further support.

Does the planner save my information?

No. Figures remain in the current browser session and are not sent to a server.
