How the estimate works

Calculation steps

1. Work out the gain

Sale proceeds minus purchase price and allowable acquisition, disposal and improvement costs.

2. Apply losses and exemption

Capital losses are deducted before the available annual exempt amount.

3. Apply the rates

Any unused basic-rate band is filled first at 18%; the remaining taxable gain is charged at 24%.

Important: This simplified estimate does not calculate Private Residence Relief, letting relief, share pooling, chattels rules, non-resident rules, carried interest, trust rates, foreign income and gains claims, or company taxation. Confirm the final treatment with HMRC guidance or a qualified adviser.
Common questions

Capital Gains Tax FAQ

What is the annual exempt amount for 2026/27?

Most individuals can use an annual exempt amount of £3,000 against eligible gains, subject to special rules and claims.

Why does taxable income matter?

Taxable income uses part of the Income Tax basic-rate band. Any remaining space can allow part of the taxable gain to be charged at 18% before the 24% rate applies.

Are residential property gains taxed differently?

For individuals from 6 April 2026, the standard rates shown here are 18% and 24%. Property reliefs and reporting deadlines can still make the calculation more complex.

How is Business Asset Disposal Relief handled?

Selecting that option applies an illustrative 18% rate to the taxable gain. Eligibility and lifetime limits must be checked separately.
