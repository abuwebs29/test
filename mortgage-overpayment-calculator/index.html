How overpayments can help

Mortgage interest is charged on the outstanding balance. Paying extra reduces that balance sooner, which can lower future interest and shorten the repayment period.

Frequently asked questions

Does this work for lump-sum overpayments?

This version models a regular monthly overpayment. A future update will add one-off lump sums.

Will my lender reduce the term automatically?

Not always. Some lenders reduce future payments instead. Ask your lender how overpayments are applied.

Can the result be exact?

No. Actual results depend on daily interest, payment dates, rate changes, fees and lender rounding.
