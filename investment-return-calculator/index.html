How it works

Understanding the estimate

Net return

The calculator subtracts annual fees from the expected annual return, then compounds the result monthly.

Fee impact

It compares the projection after fees with the same investment path before fees.

Real value

The final amount is discounted by your inflation assumption to estimate future purchasing power in today's terms.

Common questions

Investment return FAQ

What return should I enter?

Use a cautious assumption that reflects the type of assets, fees and time horizon you are considering. Past performance does not predict future returns.

Are taxes included?

No. Tax can depend on whether investments are held in an ISA, pension or taxable account and on your individual circumstances.

Why are monthly contributions compounded?

Each contribution has a different amount of time to grow. The calculator adds contributions monthly and compounds the balance each month.

Can the result be negative?

Yes. A sufficiently negative assumed return can produce a final value below the amount contributed.
