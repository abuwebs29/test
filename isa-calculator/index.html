How it works

ISA projection assumptions

Annual allowance

The calculator compares the new lump sum plus 12 monthly contributions with the £20,000 overall ISA limit.

Compound growth

The balance grows monthly using the annual rate entered, with contributions added at the end of each month.

Tax-free wrapper

The result illustrates growth inside an ISA and does not calculate tax that might apply outside one.

Important: This is a planning illustration, not financial advice. Investment returns can fall as well as rise, Cash ISA rates may change, and provider fees or account rules can affect results.
Common questions

ISA Calculator FAQ

What is the ISA allowance for 2026/27?

The overall adult ISA subscription limit is £20,000 for the tax year running from 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027.

Can I split the allowance between different ISAs?

Yes. You can divide the overall allowance across eligible ISA types, subject to account and provider rules.

Does the calculator include Lifetime ISA bonuses?

No. It models ordinary contributions and growth only. Lifetime ISA bonuses, withdrawal charges and its separate £4,000 limit are not included.

Does it predict Stocks and Shares ISA returns?

No. The entered return is a constant assumption for illustration. Actual investment performance is uncertain.

Are my figures stored?

No. Everything is calculated locally in your browser.
