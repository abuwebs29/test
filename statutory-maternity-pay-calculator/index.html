How the estimate works

2026/27 Statutory Maternity Pay rules

First 6 weeks

90% of average weekly earnings, with no statutory weekly cap.

Remaining 33 weeks

£194.32 per week or 90% of average weekly earnings, whichever is lower.

Earnings threshold

Average weekly earnings must normally be at least £129 for SMP eligibility.

Important: This calculator estimates gross statutory pay before Income Tax, National Insurance and pension deductions. Eligibility also depends on employment history and notice requirements.
Common questions

Statutory Maternity Pay FAQ

Is maternity pay taxed?

Yes. Statutory Maternity Pay is treated as earnings and is normally subject to PAYE deductions.

Can an employer pay more than SMP?

Yes. Some employers offer enhanced contractual maternity pay on top of the statutory minimum.

What happens if average weekly earnings are below £129?

You may not qualify for SMP, but you may be able to claim Maternity Allowance depending on your circumstances.
