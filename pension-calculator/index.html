Important limits

How to interpret the estimate

Annual allowance

The standard pension annual allowance is £60,000, but tapered and money purchase annual allowance rules can reduce it.

Auto-enrolment minimums

Many schemes use 5% employee and 3% employer minimum contributions on qualifying earnings, not necessarily full salary.

Access age

Most people cannot normally access private pension benefits before the normal minimum pension age, which rises to 57 on 6 April 2028.

Planning illustration only: This tool models a defined-contribution pot using full salary percentages. It does not calculate tax relief mechanics, qualifying earnings, salary sacrifice savings, defined-benefit pensions, State Pension, tax on withdrawals or provider-specific rules.
Common questions

Pension Calculator FAQ

Does this include State Pension?

No. It estimates a private defined-contribution pension pot only.

Are contributions based on full salary?

Yes. For simplicity, this calculator applies the entered percentages to full annual salary. Many workplace schemes instead use qualifying earnings.

What does the 4% draw figure mean?

It is a simple illustration equal to 4% of the projected pot. It is not a recommendation and does not guarantee the pot will last.

Does it include tax relief?

The employee percentage is treated as the total amount entering the pension from the employee side. The calculator does not separately model relief-at-source, net pay or salary sacrifice.

Are my figures stored?

No. The calculation runs locally in your browser.
