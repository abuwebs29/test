How it works

Rates and thresholds used

This calculator uses simplified annualised category A employee thresholds for 2026/27.

Below £12,570

No employee National Insurance is estimated below the annualised Primary Threshold.

£12,570 to £50,270

Earnings in this range are charged at 8% in this estimate.

Above £50,270

Earnings above the annualised Upper Earnings Limit are charged at 2%.

Common questions

National Insurance Calculator FAQ

Which National Insurance category does this calculator use?

It uses employee category A rates. Other category letters can produce different deductions.

Does this include employer National Insurance?

No. It estimates employee Class 1 National Insurance only.

Why might my payslip differ?

Payroll normally calculates National Insurance for each pay period. Rounding, bonuses, irregular earnings, category letters and multiple jobs can all affect the result.

Does this include Income Tax?

No. Use the Salary Calculator or Income Tax Calculator for tax estimates.

Are my pay details stored?

No. The calculation runs locally in your browser and does not require an account.
