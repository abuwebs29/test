Estimate only: this calculator assumes employee National Insurance category A and smooths the result across a year. Actual payroll deductions are calculated by pay period and may differ slightly because of rounding, irregular pay or a different category letter.

Rates used

For 2026/27, category A employees pay 8% on earnings between the Primary Threshold and Upper Earnings Limit, then 2% above the Upper Earnings Limit. The annualised thresholds used here are £12,570 and £50,270.

Who may get a different result?

Directors, employees with category letters other than A, people with multiple jobs, and workers with irregular bonuses can have different National Insurance calculations.
