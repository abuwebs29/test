How the estimate is built

SDLT is calculated marginally, so each rate applies only to the portion of the price inside that band.

Current residential bands

Standard SDLT rates from 1 April 2025

Up to £250,000

0% on the first £125,000, then 2% on the portion from £125,001 to £250,000.

£250,001 to £1.5m

5% from £250,001 to £925,000, then 10% from £925,001 to £1.5 million.

Above £1.5m

12% applies to the portion above £1.5 million.

Scope: This calculator covers straightforward residential purchases in England and Northern Ireland. It does not calculate Scotland's LBTT, Wales's LTT, lease rent tax, shared ownership elections, linked transactions, corporate 17% rules, mixed-use property or specialist reliefs.

Common questions

Stamp Duty Calculator FAQ

Does this cover Scotland or Wales?

No. Scotland uses Land and Buildings Transaction Tax, while Wales uses Land Transaction Tax.

When can first-time buyer relief apply?

It may apply when every buyer has never owned residential property anywhere in the world, intends to occupy the property as a main residence and pays no more than £500,000.

What happens above the £500,000 first-time buyer limit?

First-time buyer relief is unavailable, so the standard residential rates apply to the entire purchase price.

When do additional property rates apply?

They usually apply to purchases of £40,000 or more when the transaction means the buyer will own more than one residential property, although replacement-main-residence and refund rules may apply.

Are my property details stored?

No. The calculation runs locally in your browser and does not require an account.
