Rates used for 2026/27

The calculator uses the standard £12,570 Personal Allowance, including its reduction by £1 for every £2 of adjusted income above £100,000. For England, Wales and Northern Ireland it applies 20%, 40% and 45% Income Tax bands. Scotland uses the 19%, 20%, 21%, 42%, 45% and 48% bands. Employee National Insurance is estimated at 8% between £12,570 and £50,270 and 2% above that.

How salary sacrifice is handled

The selected pension percentage is deducted from gross salary before Income Tax and National Insurance are estimated. Actual workplace pension arrangements may work differently, so compare the result with your payslip or payroll provider.

Frequently asked questions

Why might my payslip differ?

Your tax code, pay frequency, student loan, pension method, benefits, bonuses and earlier pay in the tax year can all change PAYE deductions.

Does this include employer National Insurance?

No. The result shows employee deductions only.

Is Scotland calculated differently?

Yes. Select Scotland to use the Scottish earned-income tax bands for 2026/27.
