Rates used

How this estimate is calculated

The calculator uses simplified annualised 2026/27 rates and assumes standard employee circumstances.

Personal Allowance

Standard allowance: £12,570.

It reduces by £1 for every £2 of adjusted income above £100,000.

Income Tax

England, Wales and Northern Ireland use 20%, 40% and 45% bands.

Scotland uses 19%, 20%, 21%, 42%, 45% and 48% bands.

Employee NI

Category A estimate: 8% between £12,570 and £50,270, then 2% above £50,270.

Common questions

Salary Calculator FAQ

Why might my payslip differ?

Your tax code, pay frequency, student loan, pension method, benefits, bonuses and earlier pay in the tax year can all change PAYE deductions.

Does this include employer National Insurance?

No. The calculator estimates employee Income Tax, employee National Insurance and the selected salary sacrifice pension only.

Is Scotland calculated differently?

Yes. Select Scotland to use Scottish earned-income tax bands for the 2026/27 tax year.

How is salary sacrifice handled?

The selected percentage is deducted from gross salary before Income Tax and employee National Insurance are estimated. Actual workplace arrangements can differ.

Are my salary details stored?

No. The calculation runs locally in your browser and does not require an account.
